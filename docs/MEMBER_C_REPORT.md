# Grounded Generation & Purchase Confidence Scoring report draft: Downstream Task, Generation and PCS

## Downstream task and generation

ShopSmart converts retrieved Amazon specification and review fragments into traceable shopping answers. Grounded Generation & Purchase Confidence Scoring implements a hybrid pipeline: a language model selects evidence and optionally paraphrases it, while rules own source attribution, answer structure, purchase confidence and recommendation verdicts. The implementation accepts existing knowledge-base dictionaries and Retrieval, Reranking & Evaluation retrieval dataclasses after text hydration.

Evidence is normalized by product ID and aspect. Product-title headers are separated from review bodies so product names do not contaminate sentiment classification. Each complete sentence becomes a fact with its original chunk ID, product ID and character offsets. Fact selection distributes context across products, source types and sentiment groups. Specifications remain distinguishable from user experience.

The grounded prompt treats query and source text as untrusted data, requests bounded JSON, preserves positive and negative evidence, and prohibits invented prices, numerical claims, citations and confidence scores. The local Ollama backend uses deterministic decoding settings with a fixed seed. The current selection protocol returns only fact IDs and exact product names; the program supplies original source sentences and citations. A separate optional rewrite stage produces one English conclusion per fact. Local NLI accepts rewrites at an entailment threshold of 0.9.

The self-check verifies every proposed fact ID, product name and source span. Without NLI, conclusions use complete original candidate sentences. With NLI, unsupported or oversized paraphrases are rejected and the original fact remains. The program resolves citations and product identity from verified evidence. Available opposing evidence can be reinserted when omitted. Model-selected facts and rule-supplemented facts are recorded separately. If the local LLM is unavailable, the application can transparently fall back to extractive evidence; strict experiment mode disables that selection fallback and records failures explicitly. Historical exact-copy protocol experiments are retained separately.

## Sentiment integration

The default English lexicon is a disclosed baseline, with local negation and explicit connection-failure handling. A Transformer text sentiment classifier is optional, while Aspect Analysis & Purchase Decision Explanation's aspect sentiment labels and signed scores take precedence. Product-level star averages are not used as aspect sentiment. The lexicon alone does not authorize an unqualified positive purchase verdict.

## PCS

PCS combines quality, opinion consistency and recency, gated by evidence volume and demand coverage. The implemented formula is 100 × V × C × (0.40Q + 0.35S + 0.25R), with V=n/(n+5) and S=1-P. Polarization is the requested-aspect weighted mean of 4p_positive p_negative; coverage is the weighted proportion of adequately supported requested aspects. Formula, thresholds and weights are implementation choices requiring validation.

Quality rewards substantive text, verified purchases and log-scaled helpful votes. Recency decays with a 180-day half-life. Missing timestamps use a disclosed neutral value; missing verified/helpful metadata earns no bonus. True source review IDs prevent repeated fragments from inflating counts. Without those IDs, normalized text units are a limited proxy, and outputs do not claim independent user counts.

PCS measures evidence confidence rather than favorable sentiment. Consistently negative evidence may have high PCS, so a separate fit score and budget check govern recommendation language. Aspect Analysis & Purchase Decision Explanation can supply aspect distributions or aggregate polarization and coverage, while concrete claims still require traceable source text.

## Implementation validation and limitations

On 2026-10-12, 32 automated tests passed, covering citation identity and offsets, fabricated facts, omitted opposing evidence, negation, evidence-volume behavior, polarization and coverage penalties, missing metadata, temporal decay, duplicated reviews, invalid signals, LLM fallback, Parquet handoff and evaluation export contracts. A real product from the delivered 4,450,749-row Parquet corpus was processed without loading BM25/dense indexes. Synthetic fixtures are explicitly labeled and used only for functional smoke tests.

On 2026-10-13, local Qwen2.5 7B and Llama 3.1 8B were integrated and exercised on the same 19 real Amazon evidence fragments. Qwen contributed six verified model claims and Llama seven; one Llama proposal was removed for failing the exact-sentence check. Both final answers had seven traceable citations and identical rule-computed PCS of 53.84. Wall times were 12.76 and 12.78 seconds including initial loading and processing. These single-case measurements verify integration, not comparative model quality. The automated suite now contains 36 passing tests.

On 2026-10-13, the local cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-small verifier was exercised on 12 developer-authored entailment, contradiction and neutral examples. All matched their expected labels in that small diagnostic; it is not a held-out accuracy estimate. Both local models were also tested with ID/name selection and English single-fact rewriting.

On 2026-10-15, the automated suite reached 47 passing tests. The generation-testing runner freezes inputs, code snapshots, model digests and configuration, records exact prompts and raw structured responses, and supports verified checkpoint recovery. The 20-question two-model development batch and its final counts are documented in outputs/member_c_generation_testing_2026-10-15/summary.md. All 791 candidate text hashes were checked, with no relevance labels used for selection or reranking. Candidate display order is not established retrieval rank, and these development queries do not constitute an independent test set. Actual prompt contexts, blinded packets, blank human scoring templates and failure records are supplied for System Integration, User Interface & Evaluation.

All 40 planned attempts completed: Qwen produced 20 answers and Llama produced 18, with Llama Q002 and Q016 failing because nine proposed selections exceeded the configured limit of eight. These failures remain in the denominator and raw records. The 38 answers contain 227 citations with matching frozen source text, product identity and character spans; 18 successful model pairs have identical input evidence, selection prompts and PCS. A separate 24-check artifact audit passed. These checks establish reproducibility and provenance, not semantic answer accuracy.

Quality review identified product/accessory mismatch, personal-experience scope loss despite NLI acceptance, lexicon sarcasm errors, HTML fragments and an output-count contract mismatch. See QUALITY_REVIEW.md in the handoff directory. Mechanical citation traceability is reported separately from semantic faithfulness. Full retrieval integration latency, PCS calibration, formal human evaluation, IAA, RAGAS and ablation performance remain unestablished by this batch. Reference answers are retained only when actually provided.

## Generation revision and paired regression (2026-10-15)

The revised implementation constructs selection and rewrite schemas from the same configured maximum used by the prompt and validator (eight in this batch). Full canonical product names remain required; only Unicode NFC, HTML entities and whitespace are normalized, without dropping model numbers or suffixes. Product types and explicit requirements/exclusions are derived from the query and supplied to selection. A conservative title-based gate runs before product ranking, fact construction and rule supplements, with excluded products logged. This gate identifies title-level type compatibility, not verified functional suitability.

Every review rewrite starts with “One reviewer reports that”. A separate conservative guard checks personal scope, explicit negation, numeric details and units, conditions, uncertainty and temporal markers. NLI then evaluates the proposition after the reporting prefix at the unchanged threshold of 0.9. Rejected rewrites retain original quotations. Rendering removes HTML fragments and attributes retained quotations without changing stored source text or citation spans. The suite contains 55 passing tests.

The final regression artifacts are in outputs/member_c_generation_testing_revised_final_2026-10-15/. The old baseline is preserved. Raw candidate evidence, query order, model weights and PCS time are fixed across runs, but filtering changes actual facts and products. This is a combined pipeline regression rather than an isolated prompt ablation. An initial diagnostic run was stopped after reporting-prefix-related NLI false rejections were identified; it remains archived separately and is excluded from the final comparison.

The guards are necessary checks, not full semantic validation. For example, revised Qwen Q004/F1 preserves a negation and an “unless” clause but weakens the source's “not as good as the SP3” comparison; NLI still accepts it. Lexicon sentiment, multi-aspect coverage, PCS calibration, independent human evaluation and formal retrieval conditions remain outstanding. See revision_comparison.md and QUALITY_REVIEW.md for measured outcomes and limitations.
